GRAPHOS (inteGRAted PHOtogrammetric Suite) is a comprehensive solution for photogrammetric processing of aerial and UAV image flights. Designed to cover the complete digital photogrammetry workflow, it allows automatic image orientation, bundle adjustment with camera self-calibration, and the generation of high-precision geomatic products: Digital Terrain and Surface Models (DTM/DSM), dense 3D point clouds, textured meshes, and orthomosaics.

Key Capabilities

  • Orientation & Self-Calibration: Accurate estimation of interior and exterior camera orientation via global bundle adjustment.
  • Dense 3D Reconstruction: High-density point cloud generation and textured 3D mesh modeling.
  • Digital Elevation Models (DTM/DSM): Raster elevation product generation tailored for cartographic and engineering applications.
  • Orthoprojection: Geometrically corrected orthophotos and high-resolution orthomosaics.
  • Flexible Execution: Accessible through an intuitive Graphical User Interface (GUI) and a Command Line Interface (CLI) for batch processing and workflow automation.
